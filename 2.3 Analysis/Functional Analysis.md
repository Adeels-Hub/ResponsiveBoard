The primary goal of functional analysis is to: Identify and understand the functions of a system or process.
Identify interactions and relationships between components.
Ensure alignment between business requirements and technical implementations.
**How to do**
- Understand the **purpose** of the analysis.    
- Identify **stakeholders** and gather **initial** requirements.    
- Define the **boundaries** and limitations of the analysis.
- Conduct **interviews**, workshops, or brainstorming sessions with **stakeholders**.    
- Gather **functional requirements,** which describe what the system should do.    
- Collect **non-functional requirements**, which include performance, security, and usability
 **Techniques:**
 - **Context Diagram**
- **User Stories:** Capture requirements from the user's perspective.    
- **Use Case Diagrams:** Visualize interactions between users and the system.    
- **Requirement Traceability Matrix (RTM):** Map requirements to functional components.
