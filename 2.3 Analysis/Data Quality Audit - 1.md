**Establish data quality dimension. What to check**
- **Completeness:** Are all necessary data fields populated?    
- **Consistency:** Is the data consistent across multiple systems?    
- **Timeliness:** Is the data up-to-date?    
- **Validity:** Does the data conform to specified formats and ranges?    
- **Uniqueness/Duplicate:** Are there duplicate records?
