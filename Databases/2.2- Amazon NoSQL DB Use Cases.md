 📘 **MongoDB Atlas Global Clusters**
- **Multi-region Writes**: ✅ Supported (configurable zones)
- **Auto Failover**: ⚠️ Partial (manual intervention may be needed depending on setup)
- **Latency Optimized**: ✅ Yes (custom shard-to-region mapping)
- **Use When**:  
  • You want true MongoDB behavior with multi-region support  
  • You’re okay using a managed third-party service (MongoDB Inc.)  
  • Your app relies on complex document queries and distributed writes

 📘 **AWS Aurora Global Database**
- **Multi-region Writes**: ❌ No (read-only replicas)
- **Auto Failover**: ✅ Yes (with setup)
- **Latency Optimized**: ✅ Yes (fast replication to read replicas)
- **Use When**:  
  • You need a relational database with global read scalability  
  • Write latency is not globally critical  
  • You want automatic failover with strong RDS/SQL support
